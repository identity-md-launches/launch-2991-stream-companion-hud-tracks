Squeekside is an independent, read-only companion for the public Squeekchain Sepolia deployment. It is not an official Squeekchain application.

Design review used the assignment's pinned Better Interface reference, adapted from Jakub Krehel's Better Interface (MIT, commit `267330e1adfc66a718fb65fa6918c1f06d0a689e`). Design documentation used the pinned adaptation of Paul Bakaus's Impeccable documentation guidance (Apache-2.0, commit `9d715cc4f5564a990ca8345abfdd5df6dc9b41c8`). The retained notices and license texts are in `public/licenses/Design-guidance.txt` and the production export's `licenses/` directory. The application, mascot, and wheel illustration were implemented for this assignment; the design guides were not copied into the application.

Inter is by Rasmus Andersson and IBM Plex Mono is by IBM. Their local Latin font subsets are distributed under the SIL Open Font License, with notices in `public/licenses/`.

The interface uses Lucide icons (ISC), React (MIT), viem (MIT), and Zod (MIT). Corresponding notices are retained in `public/licenses/` and copied by the build into `dist/licenses/`.
